title: A welcome, and Getting started with Pi
date: 2026-10-28
big: true
---

- **Welcome:** a new pi-phone opens with three short steps, each one skippable: the app's language, signing in to a
  model, and the phone's permissions (in a browser: notifications and installing the app). Settings: `onboarding.welcome`.
- **Getting started:** Menu › Getting started opens a session where Pi asks what you want to cover, shows you only
  that, changes settings on cards you approve, and ticks a checklist under the session's header. Open it again any time.
- **Cards that open a screen:** when you must act yourself (Android's Accessibility, the microphone, a sign-in), Pi
  puts a card with an Open button in the chat; nothing opens until you tap it.
- **Dictation's language** now goes for all your devices, and Pi can set it (`dictation.language`). Pi can also turn
  a browser's notifications off (`notifications.device`).
- **What's new:** after a big update like this one, a card on the home screen and the session list offers a tour.
